TITLE
The Aswang: Where the Philippine Legend Really Began | Papel Tales

DESCRIPTION
Where does the aswang come from? Lolo Kanor the Philippine eagle tells little Pina the mouse-deer the real history of the Philippines' most famous night creature, from Spanish records of 1582 and 1589, to the village tiktik bird, to how Capiz got its reputation, to a 1950s psychological warfare story, and what folklorists say the legend really means.

A paper-cut story in mixed cut-out styles: cardboard, crayon, watercolour, shadow-puppet silhouettes, wood grain and gritty grey cut-outs.
