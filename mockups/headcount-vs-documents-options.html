Headcount against documents: four alternatives

Today's panel draws headcount and documents as two bars side by side, but they use different scales (1,400 people vs 640,000 documents), so the lengths cannot be compared. Each option below shows the relationship directly. All four are native Power BI visuals. Figures are the prototype's sample data.

One bar per department, sorted. The green line is the bank average (296 documents per person).
Recommended. One number answers the question: who uses EDRMS more or less than their size suggests. Power BI: clustered bar chart plus a constant line.
