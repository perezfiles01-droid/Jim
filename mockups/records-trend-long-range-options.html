Records declared over time: handling ten years of data

Illustrative figures for 2025 to 2034, made up to show the layout. Today's panel puts every period on one axis; at ten years that is 120 monthly bars, too thin to read or label. Each option below keeps the chart readable however many years there are. All three are native Power BI features.

AYears first, click a year to see its months
Recommended. The default view is always one bar per year (10 bars at ten years, 20 at twenty). Clicking a bar opens that year's 12 months, and a breadcrumb leads back. Power BI: drill down on a date hierarchy, the standard behaviour of a column chart.
