Rules for the People tab (whole file refused if broken)

  • Email required, a real address, each person once.
  • Only ADB mailboxes count (adb.org, adbi.org, afe.adb.org). Service and test accounts in your file (44, e.g. onmicrosoft.com, no email) are skipped and listed, not counted.
  • Department required; Office Location required.
  • Staff type: Staff, Consultant or Contractor.
  • Completed EDRMS training: Yes, No or blank (blank counts as No).
  • Pasting the raw AP pivot is accepted too: blank cells are filled down from the row above and "Total" rows are dropped automatically.

Across the tabs

  • Every unit on Departments must appear on Sites or People.
