=== SLIDE 14 ===
14
EDRMS insights dashboard key views
Key View | Answers the Question | Output | Action
Bank-wide Oversight | How is EDRMS performing across ADB? | Bank-wide EDRMS adoption, usage, compliance, and trends across departments ADB | Identify departments requiring support, monitor overall adoption, generate required reports.
Department Insights | How is a specific department managing its records? | Detailed view of a department's profile, sites and users activities, records compliance, conventions, and disposal readiness. | Follow up with focals, address compliance gaps, schedule training, review conventions, plan records management activities.
Project Insights | Are project sites being used and maintained appropriately? | Site usage, records compliance and other information for Sovereign and Nonsovereign project sites. | Monitor recordkeeping practices, support project teams.
Institutional File Plan Insights | Are users classifying records correctly? | Visibility on how File Plan terms are being applied across sites and libraries. | Identify underused terms, validate file plan application, improve site design, update conventions if needed.
Retention and Disposal Insights | What records require action under the RRDS? | Records due for disposal, retention label application, disposal status and compliance. | Initiate disposal reviews, monitor retention compliance, prioritize departments with upcoming or overdue disposal actions.
Records and Archive Holdings | What records and archives are being managed by RAC? | Physical records holdings, archival collections, transfers to RAC, and retrieval activity. | Plan storage requirements, monitor transfer activities, support retrieval requests, identify records for archival review.
